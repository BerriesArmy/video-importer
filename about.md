# Video Importer

Turns a GIF, a picture or a folder of frames into animated pixel art inside the level editor. Open the editor pause menu and press the Video button.

The picture is drawn with a grid of blocks. Each block has its own color channel, and a spawn trigger plays each frame by changing only the blocks that differ from the frame before. That keeps the trigger count down compared with redrawing everything every frame.

After the import finishes, a small window lets you add the usual extras: hide the player, keep the video on screen, loop it, end the level when it finishes, or switch to a black background.

Placement can be a background behind normal gameplay or a movie in front. Grid size, frame rate, position and the color channel and group ranges are in the mod settings.

This mod was written with the help of AI and published through the Vibemodded Index.
